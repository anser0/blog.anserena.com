i've moved to nyc for grad school at columbia!

- it was a bit overwhelming at first, but now i feel like i actually live here. i've been really happy these days: exploring, enjoying the nice weather, meeting new people, having friends visit, and coincidentally bumping into people from my hometown on the street.
- it's nice to see so many people outdoors on campus, because the buildings aren't connected like mit, and it's more compact.
- this semester i'm taking algebraic topology, complex analysis, and lie algebras. our first year is mostly classes, seminars, and working in the calculus help room for 2 hrs/week.
- i like how the math building is just called "mathematics." same for the philosophy building, but everything else is named after someone.
- after subtracting my rent from my stipend, i can spend <span>$</span>70 per day (or <span>$</span>90 for the 3 years i have nsf), which is more than enough to eat well and have fun in nyc. :)

some things i've done so far:

- i got rollerblades! i used to have rollerblades in elementary school, but then outgrew them.
it's actually much scarier than i remember, although i do have a helmet and pads.
so far i've only skated nearby and practiced braking and falling, but i plan to go to community skate events soon!
- joined the latin dance club which has salsa/bachata lessons every week, since i had fun salsa dancing in mexico in january. it's actually affiliated with the med school, which means i fit in better age-wise.
- i've been reading more. recently read <i>the hardest ones to fool</i> by christina li (my favorite author).
- watched niu lai at a bar and found it hilarious. probably my favorite movie experience ever, and it helped that it was at 1.5x speed and with lots of other people.
- went birding for the first time in prospect park and greenwood cemetery, which has the tallest natural point in brooklyn (battle hill), and a big parakeet nest on the gothic archway.
- went to moma (free for columbia students) in the last hour they were open, which meant no crowds.
